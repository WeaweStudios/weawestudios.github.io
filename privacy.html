1. Who we are

Nova Rush ("the game") is developed and published by Baturalp Özgül, an individual developer based in Türkiye who publishes games under the name Weawe Studios ("we", "us"). Baturalp Özgül is the controller of the personal data described here (the "veri sorumlusu" under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, KVKK). Contact: weawestudios@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. On first launch the game creates an anonymous account so your progress can be saved in the cloud.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game can record anonymous play statistics (for example the wave where a run ended, how long a session lasted and which cards you picked). In the App Store version it is off until you agree, wherever you live: the game asks you once, and nothing is recorded unless you choose Share. You can switch it on or off at any time in Settings > Privacy.
  • We use Google services (Firebase and AdMob) to save progress, measure how the game is played, find crashes and show ads, and Apple's App Store to process purchases.
  • Ads are personalised only if you allow it (Apple's tracking prompt and, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form).
  • You can delete your account and data at any time: in the game under Settings, or on our account deletion page.
  • We do not sell your personal data for money.

3. What we collect and why

3.1 Game account (Firebase Authentication)

When you first open the game, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous account with a random user ID. No name or email is needed. Your Player ID (shown in Settings > Account) is derived from this account and is what we ask for in support and deletion requests. Firebase Authentication also logs IP addresses for security for a short period.

3.2 Sign in with Apple or Google (optional)

If you choose Link account in Settings and sign in with Apple or Google, the sign-in happens on Apple's or Google's own screen; we never see your password. We receive an account identifier from that provider and, if the provider shares it, an email address (with Sign in with Apple you can choose to share a private relay address instead of your real one). We use this only so you can recover your progress on another device and so we can find your account if you ask us to delete it. The sign-in itself is covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

3.3 Cloud save (Cloud Firestore)

Your save is stored in Cloud Firestore under your user ID: progress (sectors, levels, unlocks), in-game currencies and items, settings, records of purchases you made (so they are never lost), timestamps and the app version. Security rules allow each player to read and write only their own save.

3.4 Anonymous play statistics

To balance difficulty and find where the game is too hard or too easy, the game can record gameplay events such as: a session starting and how long it lasted; a run starting and ending (sector, wave reached, whether it was a win, a death or a quit, what dealt the final hit, run length, ship, level and number of kills); which level-up cards you picked; chests opened; ad offers shown or accepted; the shop being opened; and tutorial hints shown or skipped.

Each event contains only the event name, a time stamp, a random ID that changes every session, the game version and game facts like the ones above. It does not contain your name, email, Player ID, advertising ID or any other device identifier, and there is no free text.

  • Where it is kept: on your device, in the game's own storage (a few small files that are rotated automatically, so old events are overwritten). In a later version, while the setting is on, the same events will also be sent to Google Analytics for Firebase (section 3.5). We will update this policy before that change ships.
  • App Store version: off until you agree, in every country. The first time you reach the main menu (after Apple's tracking prompt and Google's ad consent form, if they appear), the game asks once whether you want to share anonymous play stats, with two equal buttons, Share and No thanks. Nothing is recorded before you choose Share; if you choose No thanks or close the game without answering, nothing is recorded. The game does not ask again after you answer, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous play stats.
  • Test versions (pre-release builds we give to invited testers, for example through TestFlight; never the App Store version): on by default, and the game shows a one-time notice about it the first time it starts, with a Don't share button. Testers can switch it off at any time in the same setting.
  • Switching it off stops new events from being recorded. Deleting your account in the game also deletes the events stored on your device, and so does uninstalling the game.

3.5 Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The App Store version will use Google Analytics for Firebase to receive the play statistics above, plus usage information it collects itself: sessions, an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate country or region derived from your IP address. It follows the same setting as the play statistics: in the App Store version it collects nothing until you choose Share, in every country, and when the setting is off, nothing is sent to Google Analytics. Analytics collection is switched off in the app itself before it first starts, so nothing is sent before your choice. Play statistics are never used for advertising: the Google Analytics advertising signals stay off whatever you choose, and the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) is never collected unless you also allow tracking (section 4).

3.6 Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config)

Some game settings (such as difficulty values, rewards and ad frequency limits) are delivered remotely so we can adjust them without an app update. This uses a Firebase installation ID and does not include your Player ID.

3.7 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes and the play statistics setting is on, Crashlytics sends a crash report: the stack trace, device model, OS version, free memory and disk space, app state at the time of the crash, and installation identifiers. We use it only to find and fix bugs. With the setting off, no crash reports are sent.

3.8 Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them to answer you.

4. Ads, tracking and consent (Google AdMob)

Nova Rush is free and is funded by ads and optional purchases. We use Google AdMob to show:

  • Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (for example a revive or double rewards);
  • Interstitial ads, shown only occasionally between runs, never during gameplay. There are no banner ads.

AdMob may collect your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and, only with your permission, your advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone; the Android advertising ID once the Android version launches). We set AdMob's content filtering so that ads suit the game's age rating.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. In these regions, before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, UMP). You can review or change your choices later in Settings > Privacy > Privacy options (this button is shown where the consent form applies).

Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT). On iPhone the game asks for permission before any data is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not available and you will see non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Even non-personalised ads use some data (for example IP address and device information) for frequency capping, measurement, and fraud and abuse prevention. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases (for example No Ads, the Starter Pack, Crystals or the Monthly Card) are processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never receive your card or payment details. We receive the transaction ID, product ID and purchase date so we can deliver the item, restore it on another device and prevent fraud. When the Android version launches, purchases there will be processed by Google Play Billing in the same way.

6. Legal bases (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK and similar laws)

  • Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)): running the game, your account, cloud saves, account linking and delivering purchases.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)): security, preventing cheating and fraud, remote settings, and, in test versions only, play statistics, analytics and crash reports (on by default with a one-time notice and the off switch described in section 3.4).
  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)): play statistics, analytics and crash reports in the App Store version, in every country (only after you choose Share), and personalised advertising and tracking (ATT and the consent form). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy without affecting earlier processing.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)): keeping purchase records where tax or consumer law requires it, and answering requests from authorities.

Data is collected electronically through the game, the stores' sign-in and purchase systems, and email you send us.

7. Who receives data

We share data only with service providers that process it for us or that you interact with: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase, Remote Config, Crashlytics, AdMob and Google Sign-In) and Apple (App Store, StoreKit and Sign in with Apple). If you allow personalised ads, Google and its ad partners may use data under their own policies; under some US state laws this may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising, which you can refuse with the ATT prompt and the privacy options in the game. We may disclose data if required by law, or as part of a transfer of the game to a new owner, who would be bound by this policy.

Provider policies: Firebase, Google, Apple.

8. International transfers

Google and Apple may process data in the United States and other countries outside your own. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions, as described in the providers' terms. For users in Türkiye, transfers abroad are made under the conditions of KVKK Article 9.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and cloud save: while your account exists. After you delete your account we delete your save within 30 days; Google removes deleted Authentication data from its systems within 180 days.
  • Play statistics on your device: until they are overwritten by newer events (the game keeps only a few small files), until you delete your account in the game or until you uninstall the game.
  • Analytics in Google Analytics: event-level data is kept for 14 months (the retention setting we use), then deleted. Aggregated reports that cannot identify you may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Firebase installation IDs: until deleted together with your account; removed within 180 days afterwards.
  • Authentication IP logs: a few weeks.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and consumer law requires.
  • Support and privacy emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Play statistics, analytics and crash reports: Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous play stats.
  • Ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): Settings > Privacy > Privacy options.
  • Tracking: allow or refuse in the ATT prompt, change later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • No Ads purchase: removes interstitial ads.
  • Delete: delete your account and data in the game or by email (section 11).

GDPR and UK GDPR (EEA and UK): you have the right to access your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to receive a copy in a portable format, to restrict or object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

KVKK (Türkiye), Article 11: you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed and to request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; to ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected; to ask for it to be deleted or destroyed under the conditions of Article 7, and for those third parties to be told about any correction or deletion; to object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. If we reject your application or do not answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu).

Other laws (for example California's CCPA/CPRA) may give you similar rights. We will not treat you differently for using them.

How to make a request: email weawestudios@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request", say what you are asking for, and include your Player ID (Settings > Account) or the email of the Apple or Google account you linked, so we can find your data. To protect your account we may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We answer free of charge as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest.

11. Deleting your data

In the game: Settings > Account > Delete my account and data. This deletes your account, cloud save, linked sign-in and the play statistics stored on your device. You can also request deletion by email. Full instructions: Delete your account & data. Deleting the app from your phone alone does not delete your cloud save.

12. Children

Nova Rush is rated 13+ on the App Store. It is not directed at children, is not listed in the App Store Kids category, and is not designed for players under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country, for example 16 in some EU countries). If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored by Google with encryption at rest. Access to each cloud save is limited to its own account by security rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

14. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and does not load third-party fonts or embeds. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

15. Changes

If we add features that change what data we collect (for example sending play statistics to Google Analytics, or Game Center leaderboards), we will update this policy before releasing them and change the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the game.
