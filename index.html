How it plays

  1. 1

    Drag to fly

    Your ship fires on its own. Keep moving to dodge, stand still to hit harder.

  2. 2

    Pick a card

    Every level-up offers new weapons, upgrades and modules. You shape the build.

  3. 3

    Evolve and fuse

    Max the right weapons to evolve them or fuse two into a hidden new one.

Hangar

6 ships, 6 ways to play

Each ship has its own passive and its own Nova, a screen-changing special you charge in battle.

  • Vanguard

    Flexible Plan. 4 cards on every level-up and an extra free reroll.

    NOVA Nova Wave: wipes enemy bullets off the screen.

  • Siege

    Iron Stance. Stand still and hit 50% harder with bigger areas.

    NOVA Fortress Dome: a shield no enemy bullet can enter.

  • Shade

    Graze. Skim past enemy bullets to charge your Nova faster.

    NOVA Phase Dash: an untouchable dash that leaves a burning trail.

  • Tempest

    Resonance. Two elements on one enemy set off an explosion.

    NOVA Static Storm: five seconds of lightning rain.

  • Hive

    Carrier. One extra weapon slot and +1 orbital or drone.

    NOVA Hive Awakening: releases 12 hunter drones.

  • Eclipse

    Mass. Double magnet range, nearby enemies slow down.

    NOVA Event Horizon: a vortex that swallows enemies and bullets.

Weapon fusion

Max two weapons. Discover what they become.

Pick weapons and modules from cards as you level up. Push the right pair to max level and they evolve or fuse into something new, freeing a weapon slot for more.

Example: max out Scatter Cannon and Orbit Blades and they fuse into Rebound Gyre: heavy blades orbit your ship, then fling out at the nearest enemy and spin back through the swarm.

Fusions start hidden as ??? in your Codex. Every new one you find is logged for good.

Bosses

Giant bosses that play fair

Every sector ends in a boss fight, with mini-bosses on the way. Red zones warn you a full second before an attack lands, so a hit is always readable.

Space roguelite

Short runs, progress that lasts

  • A run takes about 12 minutes. Every run earns permanent power, gear and upgrades.
  • Sectors change the rules: new enemies, traps and hazards, not just more monsters.
  • Past the last sector, endless Deep Space keeps getting harder.
  • Fair and relaxed: the whole game can be finished without paying.
