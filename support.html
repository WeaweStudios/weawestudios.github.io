Support & FAQ

Answers to the most common questions. If you still need help, email us and we will get back to you.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Contact support

Email weawestudios@gmail.com. To help us answer faster, include:

  • your Player ID (Settings > Account in the game),
  • your iPhone model and iOS version,
  • what happened and roughly when (date and time).

We usually reply within 2 working days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I keep my progress when I change phones?

Your progress is saved to the cloud automatically. To move it to another device, link your account first: open Settings > Account > Link account and sign in with Apple or Google. On the new phone, install Nova Rush, open the same screen and sign in with the same account. Your progress comes back.

Example: you are on Sector 8 on your old iPhone. You link it with Sign in with Apple. On your new iPhone you choose Sign in with Apple and continue from Sector 8.

Can I move my progress from iPhone to Android?

Yes, once the Android version launches. Link your account with Apple or Google on the iPhone, then sign in with the same account on Android. Purchases are tied to each store, so items bought on the App Store are restored on iPhone, and Google Play purchases on Android; progress and in-game currencies move with your account.

The game shows two different saves. Which one is kept?

If your device and the cloud have different progress, the game shows both (for example "Sector 8, Pilot level 24" and "Sector 3, Pilot level 9") and you choose. Purchases are never lost; they can always be restored from the store.

I deleted the game and lost my progress.

If you had linked your account, reinstall the game and sign in with the same Apple or Google account. If you never linked it, the anonymous account cannot be recovered automatically. Email us with your old Player ID if you have it, and roughly when you last played; we will check what we can do.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings > Account > Restore Purchases while signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Permanent items such as No Ads come back. Consumables (like crystals) are already in your cloud save and return when you sign in to your linked account.

I watched a rewarded ad but did not get the reward.

The reward is given when the ad finishes. If you closed the ad early or lost connection, the reward may not be granted. If you are sure you watched it to the end, email us with the date and time and your Player ID, and we will check.

Can I remove ads?

Yes. The No Ads purchase removes interstitial ads completely and gives you rewarded-ad rewards without watching an ad. There are never banner ads.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. You can play without internet; progress is saved on the device and synced to the cloud when you are back online. Ads, purchases and account linking need a connection.

How do I change the language?

The game uses your phone's language automatically. You can change it any time in Settings > Language.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

For tracking, go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also change your ad consent from the privacy options in the game's Settings. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the game: Settings > Account > Delete my account and data, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.
