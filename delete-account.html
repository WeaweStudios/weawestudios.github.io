Delete your account & data

You can delete your Nova Rush account and all game data linked to it at any time, inside the game or by email.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This page applies to the Nova Rush game (developer: Weawe Studios (Baturalp Özgül)) on iPhone and, once released, on Android.

Option 1: delete in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Nova Rush.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear button on the main menu).
  3. Open Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account and data.
  5. Read what will be deleted and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and the game starts fresh. Deleting the app from your phone alone does not delete your account.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you no longer have the game installed, email weawestudios@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Nova Rush account" and include one of the following so we can find your account:

  • your Player ID (shown in Settings > Account), or
  • the email address of the Apple or Google account you linked to the game.

We may ask you to confirm the request from that email address so nobody else can delete your account. We complete requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your game account (anonymous user ID and any linked Sign in with Apple or Google identity).
  • Your cloud save: progress, unlocks, in-game currencies and items, settings.
  • The anonymous play statistics stored on your device.
  • Analytics data linked to your app instance, where Google's tools allow deletion by user.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records we must keep for tax and consumer law (transaction ID, product, date), for as long as the law requires. Apple keeps its own purchase history under your Apple ID.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Backups: Google removes deleted data from its backups within 180 days.
  • Aggregated statistics that cannot identify you (for example "how many players beat Sector 1").

Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent. Progress and in-game currencies cannot be restored afterwards.
  • Unspent Crystals and any remaining Monthly Card days are lost. The Monthly Card is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel in iOS.
  • Permanent purchases such as No Ads stay with your Apple ID and can be restored if you play again.
