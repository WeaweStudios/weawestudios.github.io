About the game

Short description

A one-thumb space roguelite: fly, evolve your weapons and outlast the swarm.

Long description

Nova Rush is a portrait space roguelite shooter played with one thumb. Drag anywhere to fly; your ship fires on its own. Waves of alien creatures pour in, and every level-up offers a choice of new weapons and modules.

Push the right weapons to max level and they evolve or fuse into new hidden weapons that fill your Codex. Pick from six ships, each with its own passive and a screen-changing Nova special, and take on giant bosses whose attacks are always clearly telegraphed.

Runs last about 12 minutes, and every run earns permanent power. Past the final sector, endless Deep Space keeps the challenge going. The game is free and can be finished without paying.

Key features

  • One-thumb portrait controls: drag to fly, auto-fire
  • 6 ships with unique passives and Nova specials
  • Weapon evolutions and hidden fusions to discover
  • Giant bosses and mini-bosses with readable attacks
  • Short 12-minute runs with lasting progression and endless Deep Space
  • Cloud save with Sign in with Apple and Google
